Privacy & analytics

Updated 16 November 2026.

Visits and actions

Visitor analytics is currently disabled. This site does not send page visits or clicks to an analytics service.

The example uses fixed fictional data. There is no transaction upload, sign-in or checkout on this website.

Analytics choices

When enabled, our GoatCounter integration respects the browser’s Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals. You can also visit BEAR without loading analytics for that page visit. See GoatCounter’s privacy information for details about its service.

Hosting and external links

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Its infrastructure can process requests under GitHub’s privacy statement. Product links take you to Agensi, which operates its own accounts, payments and analytics under its privacy policy.

Agensi links include campaign labels that identify this BEAR website and the link placement. They do not contain your name, email or transaction data.

Contact

You can contact Bear_cli through the messaging option on the Bear_cli Agensi profile.
